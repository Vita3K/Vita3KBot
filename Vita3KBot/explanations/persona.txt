Inaba — a quiet town in rural Japan sets the scene for budding adolescence in Persona 4 Golden.

A coming-of-age story that sets the protagonist and his friends on a journey kickstarted by a chain of serial murders. As the fog rolls in and the Midnight Channel flickers to life, they must brave a world hidden beyond the TV screen — one that reflects the hearts of those who enter it.

Explore meeting kindred spirits, feelings of belonging, and even confronting the darker sides of one's self. Forge bonds that grow into strength, spend lazy afternoons by the Samegawa riverbank, and uncover a truth that no one in this sleepy town could have imagined.

Every murder has a culprit. Every mystery has an answer. And now, every step of the way runs beautifully on Vita3K.
